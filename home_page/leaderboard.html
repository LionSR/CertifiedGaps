Each row is a theorem of the form: for every ring length $L$ in the stated range, the Hamiltonian has a unique ground state and a spectral gap $\Delta_L = E_1(L) - E_0(L)$ at least the stated bound. Bounds are proved by the spacetime bootstrap from a single seed $(n_0, \beta_0)$; decimals are rounded down (open a bound to see its exact form). The tier says how the theorem is verified. The data live in records.yml; contributions are welcome.

Assumed and conditional results

These results use the same criterion but rest on inputs this repository does not reproduce (tier assumed): estimates imported from another paper, or finite tables whose certification is still open. They are listed for completeness and are not counted as certified. Conditional Lean records are different: there the only hypotheses are the two seed inequalities, and those are certified by computer in the repository (see tiers).

Tiers

Badges differ in glyph, label and border, not only in color.

{% include tier-badge.html tier="lean-unconditional" %}
A Lean theorem, checked by the Lean kernel, with no hypotheses: for every even $L \ge L_0$ the stated Hamiltonian has a unique ground state and $\Delta_L > \log(r)/\beta$. Its axioms are among propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound (no sorry, no native_decide), and the seed itself is verified inside the kernel.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="lean-conditional" %}
The same Lean statement, proved from exactly two hypotheses: the seed inequalities $P_{\mathrm{s}}(n_0, \beta_0) \ge 1 - \varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}}$ and $P_\tau(2n_0, \beta_0) \ge 1 - \varepsilon_\tau$ for that Hamiltonian. Reflection positivity, the doubling recurrence and the arithmetic of the bound are proved in Lean; the two seed inequalities are certified by computer in models/<slug>/, so the result is as reliable as that certificate's trusted base. The “Lean record” column shows the Lean bound, which may be weaker than the certificate's bound in the “certified gap” column.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="certified" %}
A rigorous computer certificate per AGENTS.md: an exact reflection-positivity identity, seed enclosures in exact, interval or verified floating-point arithmetic with an explicit rounding analysis, and the certified recurrence, all recomputed by the model's check.jl. No Lean record yet.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="unreviewed" %}
A certificate and checker exist, but the computation has not yet been independently reviewed.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="assumed" %}
The proof relies on estimates imported from elsewhere (for example the four finite estimates of the boundary-field paper) or on finite tables not yet certified here.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="target" %}
Floating-point survey estimates only; no rigorous bound.

For rows with a Lean record, the tier is taken from lean_records.json, which continuous integration regenerates on every build by auditing the Lean library Leaderboard (its axioms and the kernel re-check); it is never read from records.yml. Known gaps are numerical estimates for comparison (finite-ring exact diagonalization, quantum Monte Carlo or exact free-fermion values) and are not certified.
