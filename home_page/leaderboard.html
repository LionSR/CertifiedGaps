Each row is a theorem: for every ring length $L$ in the stated range, the Hamiltonian has a unique ground state and a spectral gap $\Delta_L = E_1(L) - E_0(L)$ at least the stated bound, proved by the spacetime bootstrap from one seed $(n_0, \beta_0)$. Bounds are rounded down; “details” gives the exact form, the Lean record and the evidence. Known gaps are floating-point or exact reference values for comparison, not certified. Tiers

A badge describes the bound printed next to it. The headline gap of a row is its best rigorous bound. When the row's Lean theorem proves at least that bound (compared in exact arithmetic by the validator), the headline badge is the Lean one; otherwise it is the tier of the computer certificate, and the Lean theorem, with its own badge, is listed under “details”. Lean kinds are read from lean_records.json, which continuous integration regenerates on every build by auditing and re-checking the Lean library Leaderboard; they are never read from records.yml.

{% include tier-badge.html tier="lean-unconditional" %}
A Lean theorem, checked by the Lean kernel, with no hypotheses: for every even $L \ge L_0$ the stated Hamiltonian has a unique ground state and $\Delta_L > \log(r)/\beta$. Its axioms are among propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound (no sorry, no native_decide); the seed is verified inside the kernel.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="lean-conditional" %}
The same Lean statement, proved from exactly two hypotheses, the seed inequalities $P_{\mathrm{s}}(n_0, \beta_0) \ge 1 - \varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}}$ and $P_\tau(2n_0, \beta_0) \ge 1 - \varepsilon_\tau$. Reflection positivity, the doubling recurrence and the arithmetic of the bound are proved in Lean; the two seed inequalities are certified by computer in models/<slug>/, so the result is as reliable as that certificate's trusted base.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="certified" %}
A rigorous computer certificate per AGENTS.md: an exact reflection-positivity identity, seed enclosures in exact, interval or verified floating-point arithmetic with an explicit rounding analysis, and the certified recurrence, all recomputed by the model's check.jl. A Lean record of the row, if any, proves a weaker bound.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="unreviewed" %}
A certificate and checker exist, but the computation has not yet been independently reviewed.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="assumed" %}
The proof relies on estimates imported from elsewhere (for example the four finite estimates of the boundary-field paper) or on finite tables not yet certified here.
{% include tier-badge.html tier="target" %}
Floating-point survey estimates only; no rigorous bound.
