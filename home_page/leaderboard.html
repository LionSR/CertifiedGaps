How the bounds are checked

Certified gap
The bound after 48 doubling steps in interval arithmetic. The seed comes from verified enclosures of ring spectra or from exact rational arithmetic. The checker of each model calculates all values again from the stored certificate.
Lean 4
The bound of a Lean 4 theorem. The theorem uses the two seed inequalities as hypotheses and does 14 exact doubling steps. This bound is usually smaller by approximately $10^{-5}$. An unconditional theorem also proves the seed inequalities in the Lean kernel.
Continuous integration
For each change, CI builds the Lean theorems and checks them again in the Lean kernel with leanchecker. It also runs the checkers of the changed models.
