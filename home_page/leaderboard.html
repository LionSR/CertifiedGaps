How the bounds are checked

Gap ≥
Each model has a checker, check.jl. It checks reflection positivity exactly, bounds the seed defects $p$ and $q$ rigorously, and does the doubling steps in interval arithmetic.
Lean 4 ≥
A Lean 4 theorem assumes the seed bounds and proves the rest. Its bound can be smaller. At a starred point, Lean 4 also proves the seed bounds.
Automatic checks
Each change to a proof or a model runs these checks again. All checks also run each week.
