We give a diagrammatic exposition of OpenAI's computer-assisted
proof of a uniform gap in the periodic spin-1 Heisenberg chain.
By reading the partition-function tensor network along space and
imaginary time, we show how bounds for finite rings imply a gap for
all sufficiently long even rings. We improve the spin-1 gap bound
and extend the argument to certify uniform gaps for seven additional
reflection-positive spin chains and ladders. We formalize the general
argument in a reusable Lean library and give a complete Lean proof
for a mixed-field Ising example.
