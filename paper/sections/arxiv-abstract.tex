We explain the computer-assisted proof of the periodic spin-1 Haldane
gap using spacetime tensor networks. Reflection positivity makes a
finite two-site spatial transfer matrix positive, allowing spatial and
thermal purities to propagate certified finite inputs to all sufficiently
large even chains. We present the coupled recurrence, its certified
spin-1 trajectory, and its extension to reflection-positive Hamiltonians,
which we apply to seven additional spin-chain and ladder models.
Reusing the finite inputs together with an existing 120-site trial state gives
$\Delta_L>0.07290$ for every even $L\ge36$. A finite-dimensional proof
supports Lean formalization; we distinguish kernel-checked steps from
the remaining integer hypotheses.
