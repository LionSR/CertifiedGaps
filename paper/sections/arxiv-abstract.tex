We give a diagrammatic exposition of OpenAI's computer-assisted
proof of a uniform gap in the periodic spin-1 Heisenberg chain.
Reading the partition-function tensor network along space and
imaginary time shows how finite-ring bounds propagate across doubled
lengths and inverse temperatures to all longer even rings. From the
same finite inputs, we sharpen the spin-1 bound to $\Delta_L>0.07290$
for all even $L\ge36$, and extend the method to seven additional
reflection-positive spin chains and ladders. We formalize the general
theorem in Lean and verify a mixed-field Ising seed in the kernel.
