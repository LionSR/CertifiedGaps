We advocate a diagrammatic style of proof and use tensor-network
identities and inequalities to present OpenAI's computer-assisted
proof of a uniform gap in the periodic spin-1 Heisenberg chain.
Reading the partition-function tensor network in the spatial and
thermal directions shows how finite-scale bounds imply a gap for all
sufficiently long even rings. We extend the argument to
reflection-positive models and certify gaps for seven other spin
chains and ladders. Reusing the original finite inputs, we improve
the spin-1 bound to $\Delta_L>0.07290$ for every even ring of
$L\ge36$ sites. We formalize the general gap argument in Lean and
provide the proofs as a reusable library.
