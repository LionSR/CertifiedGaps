# Two-leg transverse-field Ising ladder: verification method and results

**Headline result:** coupled half-length seeds at $n_0 = 6$ rungs (complete verified 6-rung spectrum, Rayleigh–Ritz lower bounds on the partition functions of the 12-rung ring) certify, for every even $L \ge 4$ rungs and with a unique ground state, $\Delta_L \ge 0.371180737$ at $g = 11/4$ (seed $(6, 69/32)$), $\Delta_L \ge 0.849386526$ at $g = 3$ (seed $(6, 29/16)$) and $\Delta_L \ge 2.546626505$ at $g = 4$ (seed $(6, 11/8)$) (section "Coupled half-length seeds" below). The earlier complete-spectrum seed $(n_0, \beta_0) = (4\text{ rungs}, 3/2)$ certifies $\Delta_L \ge 0.636870849$ at $g = 3$ (interval bound; exact chain bound $\Delta_L > 0.636342898$) and carries the seeds of the Lean theorem. Fields $g = 2$ and $5/2$ have no admissible seed with $n_0 \le 4$ rungs, and $g = 5/2$ none at $n_0 = 6$ with coupled defects.

## Model and scope

Each unit cell is a two-spin rung ($d = 4$). In Pauli normalization on a periodic ring of $L$ rungs,

```math
H_L = -\sum_{j=1}^L (Z_{j,a} Z_{j+1,a} + Z_{j,b} Z_{j+1,b} + Z_{j,a} Z_{j,b}) - g \sum_{j=1}^L (X_{j,a} + X_{j,b}).
```

The $L = 2$ ring includes both periodic leg bonds, following the core package convention. For every $g > 0$, the off-diagonal terms in the $\sigma^z$ basis form a connected hypercube graph with negative edge weights $-g$, so Perron–Frobenius applied to $cI - H_L$ guarantees a unique ground state on every finite ring (while the two-purity doubling criterion establishes the uniform infinite-volume gap and ground-state uniqueness).

In this Pauli normalization, DMRG finite-size scaling by Xavier, Pereira, Nunes, and Plascak ([arXiv:2111.11404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11404), Eq. (1) and Table I with $J_\perp^x = 0$, $J_\perp^z = 1$, $J_4 = 0$) locates the quantum critical point at $g_c = 1.83214(5)$, placing all four tested fields $`g \in \{2, 5/2, 3, 4\}`$ in the gapped paramagnetic phase. This literature value is used only for physical context and never enters the certificate.

## Exact frame, reflection positivity and symmetry decomposition

In the real $\sigma^z$ cell basis $(\uparrow\uparrow, \uparrow\downarrow, \downarrow\uparrow, \downarrow\downarrow)$ with $Z = \mathrm{diag}(1, -1)$ and $`X = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)`$, the physical bond satisfies $-h = (Z \otimes I) \otimes (Z \otimes I) + (I \otimes Z) \otimes (I \otimes Z)$ with a real symmetric on-site rung operator $o = -Z \otimes Z - g(X \otimes I + I \otimes X)$; this is the form of the Lean model. The transverse field is not diagonal in this basis, so the certificate uses the rotated frame of a Hadamard gate on every spin: $W = (H \otimes H)/2$ with $`H = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)`$ is an exact rational orthogonal involution on a rung with $W X W = Z$ spin by spin. The rotated ladder `Ladder.ladder(g)` has

```math
h = -(X_a X_{a'} + X_b X_{b'}), \qquad o = -X_a X_b - g (Z_a + Z_b), \qquad -h = C_1 \otimes C_1 + C_2 \otimes C_2,\quad C_1 = X \otimes I,\ C_2 = I \otimes X,
```

so it is reflection positive with real factors and $F = I$. The core `is_frame_change` checks the frame identities between `Ladder.physical_ladder(g)` and `Ladder.ladder(g)` (in `check.jl` and the tests), and the tests check both RP identities over `Rational{BigInt}`; the core `verify_rp` checks the rotated one again inside `certify_point` and `check_point`.

In the rotated frame the number of down spins mod 2 is a conserved additive charge (`charge = [0, 1, 1, 0]`, `modulus = 2`): every $X$ flips one spin and every term flips two. The leg exchange $S$ ($\uparrow\downarrow \leftrightarrow \downarrow\uparrow$, `LEG_SWAP`) is a real permutation involution on a rung that commutes with $h$, $o$ and the charge. The core `VerifiedSpectra.ring_blocks` splits the ring exactly into charge sectors, lattice momenta $k = 2\pi m/L$ ($0 \le m \le L/2$; the model is real, so $\pm k$ are merged with multiplicity 2) and the parity of $S^{\otimes L}$, using symmetrized orbit states of the group generated by translations and $S^{\otimes L}$. The lattice reflection $j \mapsto -j$ maps each rung to itself and the bond to its mirror image, so the model declares it with the trivial on-site map (`reflection = I`, checked exactly by `RingModel`); since the model is real, the core uses it to build every momentum block, including $0 < k < \pi$, as a real symmetric matrix. Block entries are enclosed in interval arithmetic (exact couplings times enclosed phases and orbit-size ratios); the Float64 center is exactly Hermitian, and the Frobenius norm of the entry radii bounds its distance to the exact block. The 4-rung ring gives 12 blocks (largest 24), the 8-rung ring 20 blocks (largest 2102), with multiplicities summing to $4^4$ and $4^8$. The test suite compares the core blocks for $L = 2, 3, 4$ with an independently constructed $4^L \times 4^L$ Hamiltonian. (The core does not split blocks by reflection parity, which the former local code also used; that would halve the $k \in \{0, \pi\}$ blocks once more.)

## Enclosure and seed arithmetic

Every block goes through `CertifiedGaps.VerifiedSpectra.verified_eigen`: with LAPACK eigenpairs $(\Lambda, X)$ of the center $M$, the residual $R = MX - X\Lambda$ and Gram defect $G = X^\dagger X - I$ are computed in `Float64` (BLAS) and bounded in Frobenius norm with the a priori rounding bound $\gamma_{N+8}$ (every block is real), valid for any summation order and FMA under the IEEE binary64 model with gradual underflow. The smaller of the Ostrowski bound and the polar-factor bound gives a radius $r$ such that the sorted eigenvalues of $M$ lie within $r$ of $\Lambda$, and Weyl adds the construction error. The largest radii are $6.1 \times 10^{-12}$ (4 rungs) and $5.7 \times 10^{-8}$ (8 rungs), against $1.5 \times 10^{-7}$ at 8 rungs with complex blocks (before the reflection was declared) and $1.5 \times 10^{-10}$ and $3.9 \times 10^{-5}$ for the former local enclosure. `log_partition_enclosure` sums the enclosed spectra in interval arithmetic; `seed_bounds` forms $1 - \exp(\log Z_{2n_0}(\beta_0) - 2 \log Z_{n_0}(\beta_0))$ and $1 - \exp(\log Z_{2n_0}(2\beta_0) - 2\log Z_{2n_0}(\beta_0))$ and rounds their upper ends up to the grid $10^{-15}$; `certify_gap` (called by `certify_point`) then screens the seed with the interval `certified_gap_bound` and builds the exact rational doubling chain.

## Validation

- **Unit tests (`test/runtests.jl`):** exact frame and RP identities at $`g \in \{2, 5/2, 3, 4\}`$; core blocks (`float_ring_spectrum`, `verified_ring_spectrum`) against independent dense ED for $L = 2, 3, 4$ (verified radii $< 10^{-9}$).
- **Fast checker (`check.jl`):** for each point of `Ladder.POINTS`, the exact Hadamard frame change and the core `check_point`: the model-local source hashes; the stored model, RP factors, seed and `params` exactly those of the point; the seed defects at most the Lean seed hypotheses (`lean_points` of `TwoLegIsing.lean`); then `check_certificate(cert; spectra = false)`, which re-verifies the RP identity and reproduces the exact chain bound and `interval_bound_decimal`.
- **Full check (`check.jl --full`):** `check_point(...; spectra = true)` recomputes every block spectrum of the 4- and 8-rung rings from the stored model and requires the fresh defect upper ends to be at most the stored seeds.
- **Coupled seeds (`check.jl`, `RitzSeeds.RITZ_POINTS`):** the same `check_point` pins, with the seed defects recomputed by `coupled_seed_bounds` from a freshly verified 6-rung spectrum and the stored 12-rung Ritz bounds (`results/partial_g*_L12.json`, SHA-256 pinned by the certificate), and the 4-rung bridge recomputed and compared with the bound; `--full` recomputes the Ritz bounds from scratch (fresh Lanczos vectors, same deterministic start). These `--full` steps were replayed for each of the three coupled points and passed (README, Reproduce).
- **Unit tests for the coupled route:** Ritz bounds of the 4-rung ring with forced Lanczos against its complete verified spectrum (lower bounds on log Z), coupled against complete-spectrum defects at the seed (2, β), and the 4-rung bridge.
- **Cross-check without the leg-exchange symmetry:** a run of `certify_gap` with charge and momentum blocks only (10 blocks of dimension about 4100 at 8 rungs, 971 s) gave $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}} \le 0.112389884123893$, $\varepsilon_\tau \le 0.051537141979371$ and interval bound $0.6368651924843131$, consistent with the certified values.

## Coupled half-length seeds at $n_0 = 6$

**Identity.** For the ring of rungs with self-adjoint transfer operator (reflection positivity)
and even $n_0$, $P_{\mathrm{s}}(n_0, \beta) \le 1$, and

```math
P_\tau(2n_0, \beta) = \frac{Z_{2n_0}(2\beta)}{Z_{2n_0}(\beta)^2}
  = \frac{Z_{2n_0}(2\beta)/Z_{n_0}(\beta)^4}{P_{\mathrm{s}}(n_0, \beta)^2}
  \ge \frac{Z_{2n_0}(2\beta)}{Z_{n_0}(\beta)^4},
```

so the seed defects need an enclosure of $Z_6$ and only lower bounds on $Z_{12}(\beta_0)$ and
$Z_{12}(2\beta_0)$ (core `Seeds.coupled_seed_defects`, `GapCertificates.coupled_seed_bounds`).

**Lower bounds on $Z_{12}$.** The 12-rung ring ($4^{12} = 16{,}777{,}216$ states) is split by
the core `ring_blocks(...; sparse = true)` into 28 real symmetric blocks (charge $q \in \{0, 1\}$,
momentum $0 \le m \le 6$, leg-exchange parity) of dimension $349{,}350$–$350{,}064$, with
interval-enclosed entries and an exact bound on the distance of the `Float64` center to the
true block (of order $10^{-11}$ in Frobenius norm). For each block, KrylovKit Lanczos
(deterministic start vector, `tol = 1e-8`, Krylov dimension 32) gives approximate eigenvectors
$V$, and `RitzBounds.ritz_upper_bounds` turns them into rigorous upper bounds
$u_i \ge \lambda_i$ on the lowest levels: a priori $\gamma_n$ bounds on the products
$\mathrm{fl}(MV)$, $V^{\mathsf T} W$ and $V^{\mathsf T} V$, a Gershgorin shift
$c \le \lambda_{\min}$, Ostrowski's theorem for the pencil $(V^{\mathsf T} M V, V^{\mathsf T} V)$,
`verified_eigen` on the small Rayleigh-quotient matrix, and the exact matrix error. By
Courant–Fischer,
$Z_{12}(\beta) \ge \sum_B m_B \sum_i e^{-\beta u_i(B)}$; omitting levels only loses weight. The
certificates bound 6 levels per charge-0 block and 3 per charge-1 block (216 levels with
multiplicity); the Ritz upper bounds exceed the Lanczos values by about $5 \times 10^{-8}$.
The stored defects are raised by $10^{-8}$ (`defect_padding`, recorded in the certificate):
the verified radii of LAPACK eigenpairs vary from run to run (presumably alignment-dependent
OpenBLAS kernels), which moved a fresh 6-rung enclosure of $g = 11/4$ by
$3 \times 10^{-11}$ above the first stored defects; with the padding, `check.jl` recomputes the
defects from fresh spectra.

**How many levels.** In the rotated frame the charge is the spin-flip parity of the physical
ladder. Charge 0 holds the ground state and the two-quasiparticle continuum, charge 1 the
one-quasiparticle band (one level per momentum and leg parity) and the three-particle states.
At the seed temperatures ($\beta_0 \Delta \approx 4.5$–$6.5$) the weight of $Z_{12}$ beyond the
ground state is essentially the bottom of the one-particle band, so a single level per sector
already gives the bound to $10^{-4}$, and 4 (charge 0) and 2 (charge 1) levels give it to
$10^{-8}$ (`results/ritz_scan_g*.json`, truncation $k = 1, \dots, K$).

**Finite bridge.** The criterion covers even $L \ge n_0 = 6$. Each coupled certificate also
stores `finite_bridge`: outward bounds (to $10^{-15}$) on the gap of the 4-rung ring from its
complete verified spectrum (`spectral_gap_enclosure`), $\Delta_4 \ge 2.20$, $2.72$, $4.78$ at
$g = 11/4, 3, 4$; since they exceed the certified bounds, every even $L \ge 4$ is covered.

**Floating-point screening** (`scripts/ritz_scan.jl`, `scripts/sectors.jl`; not certified).
An independent sparse builder in the physical frame (group of rung translations, leg exchange
and global spin flip, $4L$ elements, complex blocks at $0 < m < 6$; checked against the core's
dense spectra at $L = 4, 6$) with KrylovKit Lanczos gives the coupled defects on a $\beta$ grid
of $1/8$, refined to $1/32$ near the optimum:

| $g$ | best $\beta_0$ | $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}}$ | $\varepsilon_\tau$ | float bound | certified bound |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5/2 | — | 0.1062 at $\beta = 5/2$ | $\ge 0.24$ on the grid | no seed | — |
| 11/4 | 69/32 | 0.049857 | 0.126479 | 0.371182 | 0.371180737 |
| 3 | 29/16 | 0.024179 | 0.075932 | 0.849388 | 0.849386526 |
| 4 | 11/8 | 0.002829 | 0.012860 | 2.546638 | 2.546626505 |

The certified defects exceed the float ones by about $10^{-7}$ (Ritz and enclosure radii), and
the bounds agree to $10^{-5}$. At $g = 5/2$ the obstacle is the coupling: at $\beta = 5/2$ the
direct thermal defect $1 - P_\tau(12, 5/2) \approx 0.054$ would be admissible together with
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{s}} = 0.106$, but its evaluation needs an upper bound on $Z_{12}(\beta)$
(the complete 24-spin spectrum), and the coupled form pays the factor
$`P_{\mathrm{s}}(6, \beta)^2 \approx 0.80`$. The 16-rung doubled ring ($n_0 = 8$, 32 spins,
blocks of $\approx 6.7 \times 10^7$ states) is beyond the memory of the core's block
construction (about 4 GB already at 12 rungs).

### Certified results

- **g = 3: Δ_L ≥ 0.636870849** (interval bound `0.6368708493341569`; exact chain bound `318171449//500000000`) for every even L ≥ 4 rungs. Seed (n0, β0) = (4, 3//2), εₛ ≤ 0.112389665926876, εₜ ≤ 0.051536217782645; bound / 8-rung ED gap = 0.239265. Certificate: `results/certificate_g3_1.json`.

Certified: the exact RP identity, whole-spectrum eigenvalue enclosures for the selected parameter/seed, outward log-partition and rational defect bounds, and the resulting uniform gap under the repository's two-purity theorem. Numerical: seed optimization, all nonverified scan spectra and finite-size gaps, the literature critical point, and comparisons to finite-ring reference gaps. The ED reference gap is not claimed to be an infinite-volume limit.
