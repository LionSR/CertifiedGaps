\chapter{Kernel-checked seeds}
\label{chap:seedkernel}

The seed inequalities carry the finite-size physics that the bootstrap cannot produce. The opt-in
library \texttt{SeedKernel} (\texttt{lake build SeedKernel}) proves seed inequalities inside the
Lean kernel: Julia generates integer witnesses (approximate eigenvectors scaled by \(2^{52}\)),
and \texttt{decide +kernel}, evaluated by GMP arithmetic in the kernel and without
\texttt{native\_decide}, checks them. This chapter covers the seed that is closed: the
longitudinal-field Ising chain at \(g = 1/4\), \(h = 1/20\), whose seed gives the first
unconditional gap theorem.

\section{Two eigenvalue bounds}

To bracket the partition functions \(Z_6(\beta)\) and \(Z_{12}(\beta)\) inside the Lean kernel
without diagonalizing \(64 \times 64\) and \(4096 \times 4096\) matrices, we combine two elementary
tools: a Ritz residual bound that encloses the two lowest eigenvalues from approximate integer
eigenvectors, and a subspace comparison lemma that bounds all remaining \(2^L - 2\) eigenvalues from
below.

\begin{lemma}[Residual bound]
  \label{lem:residual}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.exists_eigenvalue_near}
  \leanok
  For a Hermitian matrix \(A\), a vector \(x\) and \(\theta \in \R\), some eigenvalue satisfies
  \((\lambda_i - \theta)^2 \|x\|^2 \le \|Ax - \theta x\|^2\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  In the eigenbasis \(y = U^\dagger x\), \(\|Ax - \theta x\|^2 = \sum_i (\lambda_i - \theta)^2
  |y_i|^2 \ge \min_i (\lambda_i - \theta)^2 \|y\|^2\), and \(\|y\| = \|x\|\).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Counting by comparison]
  \label{lem:counting}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.card_eigenvalues_lt_le}
  \leanok
  Let \(A\) be Hermitian, \(B\) a set of basis vectors and \(\rho \in \R\). If
  \(\operatorname{Re}\langle x, Ax \rangle \ge \rho \|x\|^2\) for every \(x\) vanishing on \(B\),
  then at most \(|B|\) eigenvalues of \(A\) lie below \(\rho\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  If more than \(|B|\) eigenvalues were below \(\rho\), their eigenvectors would span a space of
  dimension larger than \(|B|\), which meets the subspace of vectors vanishing on \(B\)
  (codimension \(|B|\)) in a nonzero \(x\) with
  \(\operatorname{Re}\langle x, Ax \rangle < \rho \|x\|^2\).
\end{proof}

\section{The Ising Hamiltonian in the computational basis}

In the \(Z\)-basis, the longitudinal-field Ising Hamiltonian is diagonal up to single-spin flips of
strength \(g\), so its quadratic form is bounded below by the classical Ising energy minus \(gL\)
and its action on bit-encoded configurations reduces to exact integer arithmetic.

\begin{lemma}[Action of the Hamiltonian]
  \label{lem:ising-action}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.diagE, SeedKernel.Ising.flipAt, SeedKernel.Ising.hamiltonian_mulVec_apply}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:ising-longitudinal}
  For the ring of Definition~\ref{def:ising-longitudinal}, with classical energy
  \(E(\sigma) = -\sum_j z_j z_{j+1} - h \sum_j z_j\) (\(z_j = \pm 1\)),
  \((H x)(\sigma) = E(\sigma)\, x(\sigma) - g \sum_j x(\mathrm{flip}_j \sigma)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Evaluate the bond and site operators on basis configurations: \(Z \otimes Z\) and \(Z\) are
  diagonal and \(X\) flips one spin.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Comparison with the classical energies]
  \label{lem:ising-classical}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.re_quadratic_ge}
  \leanok
  \uses{lem:ising-action}
  For \(g \ge 0\), \(\operatorname{Re}\langle x, Hx \rangle \ge \sum_\sigma (E(\sigma) - gL)
  |x(\sigma)|^2\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Bound each off-diagonal term by \(\operatorname{Re}(\bar a b) \le (|a|^2 + |b|^2)/2\); every
  configuration has \(L\) flip neighbors.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Bridge to the integer checker]
  \label{lem:ising-bridge}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.encode, SeedKernel.Ising.applyH_encode, SeedKernel.Ising.dot_self_eq,
    SeedKernel.Ising.residualSq_eq}
  \leanok
  \uses{lem:ising-action}
  Encoding configurations as the bits of a natural number (a spin flip is \(\mathrm{xor}\,2^j\)),
  the checker's integer action of \(20 H\) and its bit-indexed sums are exactly the action of
  \(H\), the squared norm and the squared residual of the decoded vector.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Rewrite the sums over \(\{0, \dots, 2^L - 1\}\) as sums over configurations through the
  encoding bijection.
\end{proof}

\section{The seed and the unconditional gap}

Combining the two Ritz enclosures with the classical-energy floor bounds \(Z_6(97/4)\),
\(Z_{12}(97/4)\), and \(Z_{12}(97/2)\) tightly enough to discharge the seed hypothesis at
\((g, h) = (1/4, 1/20)\), giving a gap theorem with no hypothesis and no \texttt{sorry} that
depends only on the standard axioms.

\begin{definition}[Low spectrum]
  \label{def:low-spectrum}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.LowSpectrum}
  \leanok
  An eigenvalue family \(e\) has low spectrum \((a_0, b_0, a_1, \rho)\) if one eigenvalue lies in
  \([a_0, b_0]\), a second one is at least \(a_1\), and all others are at least \(\rho\).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Partition functions from a low spectrum]
  \label{lem:low-spectrum-z}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.sum_exp_ge, SeedKernel.Ising.sum_exp_le}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:low-spectrum}
  For \(\beta \ge 0\) and \(N\) eigenvalues in total,
  \(e^{-\beta b_0} \le \sum_i e^{-\beta e_i} \le e^{-\beta a_0} + e^{-\beta a_1} +
  N e^{-\beta \rho}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Keep one term for the lower bound; bound the terms termwise for the upper bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Low spectra of the seed rings]
  \label{lem:ising-low-spectra}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.lowSpectrum_of_checks, SeedKernel.Ising.lowSpectrum6,
    SeedKernel.Ising.lowSpectrum12, SeedKernel.Ising.allChunks12_eq_range}
  \leanok
  \uses{lem:residual, lem:counting, lem:ising-classical, lem:ising-bridge, def:low-spectrum}
  At \(g = 1/4\), \(h = 1/20\), the rings of \(6\) and \(12\) sites have low spectra with
  eigenvalue radius \(10^{-10}\) around the two Ritz values of the generated trial vectors, and
  anchor \(\rho = c/20 - L/4\), where exactly two classical configurations have \(20 E < c\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  The kernel checks the norms and residuals of two integer trial vectors on each ring (for
  \(12\) sites in \(64\) chunks, whose coverage of \(\{0, \dots, 4095\}\) is proved) and the
  count of classical configurations below the cutoff. Lemma~\ref{lem:residual} turns the
  residuals into eigenvalue enclosures, and Lemmas~\ref{lem:counting}
  and~\ref{lem:ising-classical} turn the classical count into the anchor.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The Ising seed]
  \label{thm:ising-seeds}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.seed_spatial, SeedKernel.Ising.seed_thermal,
    SeedKernel.Ising.first_point_seed}
  \leanok
  \uses{lem:ising-low-spectra, lem:low-spectrum-z, def:rp-purities, def:ising-longitudinal}
  For \(H_L = -\sum_j Z_j Z_{j+1} - \tfrac14 \sum_j X_j - \tfrac1{20} \sum_j Z_j\),
  \[
    \Ps(6, 97/4) \ge 1 - 9064 \cdot 10^{-9}, \qquad \Pt(12, 97/4) \ge 1 - 3141 \cdot 10^{-8}.
  \]
  These are the seed inequalities of the first grid point of
  Definition~\ref{def:ising-longitudinal}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Lemma~\ref{lem:low-spectrum-z} with the low spectra of Lemma~\ref{lem:ising-low-spectra} gives
  \(Z \ge e^{-\beta(\theta_0 + r)}\) and \(Z \le e^{-\beta(\theta_0 - r)}(1 + T)\) with \(T\)
  exponentially small; the ratios \(Z_{12}(\beta)/Z_6(\beta)^2\) and
  \(Z_{12}(2\beta)/Z_{12}(\beta)^2\) are then bounded below by \((1 - x)/(1 + t)^2\) using
  \(e^{-14} \le 2.7182818283^{-14}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An unconditional spectral gap]
  \label{thm:ising-gap}
  \lean{SeedKernel.Ising.firstPoint_mem, SeedKernel.Ising.gap_first_point,
    SeedKernel.Ising.ising_gap}
  \leanok
  \uses{thm:ising-seeds, lem:ising-longitudinal-valid, thm:rp-gap}
  For every even \(L \ge 6\), the Ising ring
  \(-\sum_j Z_j Z_{j+1} - \tfrac14 \sum_j X_j - \tfrac1{20} \sum_j Z_j\) has a unique ground state
  and
  \[
    \Delta_L > \frac{\log 13480}{97/4} \approx 0.3921 .
  \]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Theorem~\ref{thm:rp-gap} at the first grid point, whose certificate is valid
  (Lemma~\ref{lem:ising-longitudinal-valid}) and whose seed is Theorem~\ref{thm:ising-seeds}. The
  theorem has no hypothesis and no \texttt{sorry}; it depends only on \texttt{propext},
  \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
\end{proof}

\section{Spin-1 seed arithmetic}

\begin{remark}
  \texttt{SeedKernel/Spin1} implements the packed spin-1 Hamiltonian action and a shifted power
  filter on \(3^n\)-lane vectors, checks them against a row-wise specification on small rings,
  and verifies integer Ritz and residual witnesses for a filtered N\'eel trial vector on rings of
  \(6\), \(8\) and \(10\) sites. These are integer facts, not yet eigenvalue theorems; the plan
  and the open obligations for a kernel-checked spin-1 seed are in \texttt{models/lean\_seeds/}.
\end{remark}
