\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:intro}

This blueprint describes the Lean~4 formalization (the libraries \texttt{CertifiedGaps} and
\texttt{SeedKernel} in the directory \texttt{lean/}) of the \emph{spacetime bootstrap}, also called
the \emph{two-purity doubling criterion}, of the main note~\cite{HaldaneNote} and the scope
note~\cite{ScopeNote}. Every statement below is linked to the Lean declaration that proves it, and
the dependency graph shows which results rest on which.

\section*{The criterion}

For a periodic ring Hamiltonian \(H_L = \sum_{j=1}^{L} h_{j,j+1} + \sum_{j=1}^{L} o_j\) of \(L\)
unit cells, write \(Z_L(\beta) = \Tr e^{-\beta H_L}\) and define the \emph{spatial} and
\emph{thermal purities}
\[
  \Ps(n,\beta) = \frac{Z_{2n}(\beta)}{Z_n(\beta)^2}, \qquad
  \Pt(n,\beta) = \frac{Z_n(2\beta)}{Z_n(\beta)^2}.
\]
A thermal purity \(\Pt(L, \beta)\) near one means that the ground state carries almost all of the
Boltzmann weight, which forces a unique ground state and bounds the spectral gap
\(\Delta_L = E_1(L) - E_0(L)\) from below whenever the purity defect \(1 - \Pt(L, \beta)\) decays
exponentially in \(\beta\). Squaring the Gibbs operator in imaginary time improves \(\Pt\) at fixed
\(L\), but advancing \(L\) and \(\beta\) together requires a spatial transfer operator. If the bond
interaction is reflection positive, \(-h = \sum_\alpha C_\alpha \otimes \overline{C_\alpha}\) in a
local basis with a real symmetric on-site term \(o\), then \(Z_L(\beta)\) is a moment sum
\(\sum_i \mu_i^L\) of a positive transfer operator at even lengths \(L\), and both purities improve
quadratically when length and inverse temperature are doubled together. Two purity bounds at a
single \emph{seed} scale \((n_0, \beta_0)\) then yield a uniform lower bound on \(\Delta_L\) for
every even \(L \ge n_0\).

\section*{What is formalized}

The blueprint follows the five layers of the formalization:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Chapter~\ref{chap:criterion}: the abstract two-purity criterion for any family of finite
    spectra whose partition functions satisfy three \emph{spatial moment inequalities}
    (Theorem~\ref{thm:gap-of-seeds}).
  \item Chapter~\ref{chap:chain}: unequal seed defects, the exact asymmetric doubling recurrence,
    and rational chain certificates checked in the kernel
    (Theorem~\ref{thm:gap-of-chain-certificate}).
  \item Chapter~\ref{chap:rings}: proof that every reflection-positive ring satisfies the spatial
    moment inequalities, via Trotterized positive semidefinite transfer matrices and a Lie--Trotter
    product formula (Theorem~\ref{thm:rp-moment-bounds}), giving the end-to-end criterion for rings
    (Theorem~\ref{thm:rp-gap}).
  \item Chapters~\ref{chap:heisenberg} and~\ref{chap:models}: the spin-1 Heisenberg ring and eight
    further model families, each a gap theorem whose only open hypotheses are its two seed
    inequalities.
  \item Chapter~\ref{chap:seedkernel}: seed inequalities proved inside the Lean kernel. For the
    longitudinal-field Ising chain at \(g = 1/4\), \(h = 1/20\), the seed is proved in the kernel,
    giving the first unconditional gap theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:ising-gap}).
\end{enumerate}

\section*{How to read the status}

A statement marked as formalized has a Lean declaration with that exact content; a proof marked as
formalized is complete in Lean. The open nodes of the dependency graph are the seed inequalities of
each model (finite numerical statements about rings of a few to a few thousand sites, closed by
\texttt{sorry} in Lean and certified by computer in \texttt{models/}). Everything upstream of them
depends only on the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound}.

\begin{remark}[Conventions]
  \label{rem:conventions}
  The spectral gap is stated through the predicate \(\GapGT(e, c)\) (Definition~\ref{def:gapgt}):
  the eigenvalue family \(e\) has a nondegenerate minimum and every other eigenvalue lies more than
  \(c\) above it. This formulation avoids sorting eigenvalues and records ground-state uniqueness
  and the gap lower bound simultaneously. Partition functions of concrete rings are defined as
  \(Z_L(\beta) = \operatorname{Re}\Tr e^{-\beta H_L}\), the real part of a complex matrix trace.
  Ring lengths count unit cells.
\end{remark}
