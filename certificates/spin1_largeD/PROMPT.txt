Goal: produce a rigorous uniform spectral-gap certificate for one 1D quantum spin model
(specified at the end), using the two-purity doubling criterion that this repo implements.

Background (read julia/README.md and outputs/thermal-mpo-gap-scope.tex, appendices D–H):
- Setup: a periodic ring of even length L, in unit cells. Partition functions are
  Z_L(β) = Tr e^{-βH_L}.
- Purities:
  - spatial purity Pₛ(n,β) = Z_{2n}(β)/Z_n(β)²;
  - thermal purity P_τ(2n,β) = Z_{2n}(2β)/Z_{2n}(β)².
- Hypothesis: the bond is reflection positive (RP). That means there is a local real basis in
  which -h_bond = Σ_k c_k C_k ⊗ C_k with c_k ≥ 0 and every C_k real, and in which the on-site
  terms are real symmetric. Then the spatial transfer operator is self-adjoint.
- Conclusion: seeds εₛ ≥ 1−Pₛ(n₀,β₀) and εₜ ≥ 1−P_τ(2n₀,β₀) give a uniform gap
  Δ_L ≥ certified_gap_bound(εₛ, εₜ, β₀).bound for every even L ≥ n₀. This function is in
  julia/src/recurrence/certified.jl and is outward rounded.
- Uniqueness: the criterion needs a unique ground state. It can never succeed for a model
  whose ring has a degenerate or quasi-degenerate ground state.
- Existing tool: julia/scripts/ed_seeds.jl shows the existing ED seed-search workflow
  (ExactDiagonalization, Seeds, Models submodules).

Rules:
1. Write everything under certificates/<slug>/: scripts, results/*.json, REPORT.md, test/.
   - Do NOT edit julia/, spin1/, spin2/, aklt/, lean/, outputs/ or any other existing file.
   - If you need a helper the core lacks, put it in certificates/<slug>/.
2. Run with `julia --project=julia certificates/<slug>/...`. Set BLAS.set_num_threads(1)
   and parallelise over sectors or parameters with threads if useful. Keep each run under
   about 45 minutes.
3. Rigor standard. A number is "certified" only if every step below holds:
   (a) RP is proved exactly:
       - write the decomposition with exact rational/algebraic coefficients;
       - check -h_bond == Σ c_k C_k⊗C_k in exact arithmetic (Rational or symbolic) for the
         stated parameters;
       - check the on-site term is real.
       rp_test (floating point) is only a sanity check.
   (b) Every symmetry sector used for Z is diagonalised densely (no Lanczos/ARPACK). Sector
       dimensions must sum to d^L, with an asserted check.
   (c) Floating-point eigenvalues are turned into enclosures with a stated, implemented bound:
       - compute the residual R = HQ − QΛ and the orthogonality defect E = QᵀQ − I with
         higher-precision accumulation (e.g. BigFloat or Double64 matmul, or a rigorous norm
         upper bound);
       - apply a Weyl/Kahan-type theorem that bounds every true eigenvalue (with multiplicity)
         by the computed ones;
       - propagate the enclosures to upper bounds on log Z and to rational upper bounds on
         εₛ, εₜ, rounded outward;
       - state the theorem used in REPORT.md.
   (d) Feed the rational εₛ, εₜ, β₀ to certified_gap_bound. Report its .bound, plus the
       validity range "even L ≥ n₀ cells" (convert to sites).
   Anything that misses one of (a)–(d) is reported as "numerical, not certified".
4. Search strategy:
   - scan seeds over n₀ ∈ {2,…,nmax} cells and β₀ on a grid, with best_seed/scan_seeds as
     in ed_seeds.jl;
   - re-run only the best seed through the rigorous path (3b–3d).
5. Deliverables:
   - certificates/<slug>/REPORT.md, containing:
     - the model and its parameters;
     - the RP basis and the exact decomposition;
     - the sizes reached and the sector dimensions;
     - a table of the best seed per parameter value;
     - the certified bound, its validity range, and runtimes;
     - an ED/DMRG reference gap and the ratio of the bound to the gap;
     - an explicit list of what is certified versus numerical.
   - results/*.json written with ThermalMPOGap.Results.write_result. Include source hashes,
     and store exact rationals as strings.
   - certificates/<slug>/test/runtests.jl, which reruns the RP identity and recomputes the
     bound from the stored rational seeds in under 2 minutes.
6. Be honest about failure. If no certificate exists at reachable sizes, say so. Then report:
   - max over the grid of min(Pₛ, P_τ) per n₀;
   - the scaling of δ(n) = E₀(2n) − 2E₀(n);
   - an extrapolated (n₀, β₀) at which a seed would appear.
   A clean negative result is a valid outcome.

Model:
slug: spin1_largeD
H = Σ_j S_j·S_{j+1} + D Σ_j (S^z_j)², with spin 1 on a periodic ring and a one-site cell.
- RP basis: Cartesian |x⟩,|y⟩,|z⟩ with G_a = iS^a real antisymmetric, so -S·S = Σ_a G_a⊗G_a.
  (S^z)² is real in this basis.
- Known (survey, numerical ED):
  - D = 4: seed (6, 3) gives a bound of 0.543842 for even L ≥ 6;
  - D = 2 and D = 1.5: no seed with n₀ ≤ 6.
- Task: find the smallest D in [1, 4] with a certified seed.
  - Use a grid of 0.25 steps, refined near the threshold.
  - Use Sz conservation, translation and spin flip to reach L = 12 (and L = 14 if feasible).
- Give a certified bound at D = 4 and at the threshold D, plus a curve of bound/Δ against D.
